## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the reverence, safety, and heartfelt hospitality inherent to serving pilgrims visiting the Two Holy Mosques. Built on an RTL-first architecture, it pairs classical Islamic cultural dignity with the efficiency and modern assurance required by tier-one transport and hospitality operations.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Warm Editorial Hospitality** and **Contemporary Heritage Minimalism**:
- **Dignity & Calm:** The visual language intentionally eschews loud, high-stress booking patterns or aggressive promotional treatments. Generous warm cream surfaces invoke peace, spiritual reassurance, and unhurried confidence.
- **Craft & Sacred Heritage:** Subtle, non-intrusive geometric 8-point star motifs, delicate brass line dividers, and satin gold metallic accents signal elite care without ostentation.
- **Hospitality & Warmth:** Tactile card elevations, soft pill badges, and deeply legible bilingual typography create an atmosphere of welcoming ease for international pilgrims, families, and tour operators navigating pilgrimage logistics.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in historic Madinah and Makkah landscapes, architectural stonework, and traditional illuminated manuscripts.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#144534`):** Rawadh Green. Deep, prestigious, and calm. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level headers, and signature institutional framing.
- **Secondary (`#C69F54`):** Desert Gold. Used for badges of distinction, VIP designations, sacred route indicators, key highlights, and active state embellishments.
- **Tertiary (`#1B5E43`):** Palm Forest Green. Used for operational statuses, confirmed bookings, vehicle tiers, and secondary accent buttons.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#192823`):** Deep Charcoal Green. Provides high-contrast readability without the cold severity of absolute black.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm Ka'bah marble cream, eliminating sterile clinical whites.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-prominence cards, elevated sheets, and popovers.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#F5F1E9`):** Subtle card troughs, input backings, and inactive segments.
- **Surface Border (`#E6DFD3`):** Fine stone-dust hairline dividers and container outlines.

### Functional Accents
- **Gold Hover / Highlight (`#D8B26E`):** Dynamic interaction highlights on dark surfaces.
- **Gold Deep Shade (`#AA8237`):** Fine text on gold badges, borders, and tactile pressed states.
- **Success / Validated (`#1D7C59`):** Manifest confirmation, passport checks, vehicle readiness.
- **Notice / Sacred Advisory (`#C88732`):** Prayer-time departure alerts and Haram transport schedules.

## Typography

The typography strategy prioritizes bilateral legibility across Arabic and Latin scripts, accommodating both local logistical teams and worldwide pilgrims.

- **Editorial Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers literary gravitas and institutional permanence. For Arabic interfaces, prioritize Arabic serif glyphs or classical Naskh characteristics (e.g., Cairo / Amiri fallbacks) that honor traditional calligraphy without compromising modern legibility.
- **Workhorse Body (`Noto Sans`):** Ensures clear scanning on handheld devices in bright sunlight during transit between Makkah, Madinah, and Jeddah. Generous line heights (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) are mandated to gracefully support Arabic vocalizations, diacritics, and descenders.
- **Operational Data & Numeric Labels (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Selected for high-density tabular schedules, PNR flight references, luxury coach seat mappings, and timekeeping. Numerals are set with proportional lining by default, switching to tabular figures in itinerary and price tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid with safe bilateral margins to ensure comfort in bidirectional environments (RTL default, LTR secondary).

- **Grid Framework:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Maximum container width is pinned at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking across long multilingual narratives.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`.
- **RTL-First Flow:** All structural flow vectors (timelines, route progressions from Jeddah Airport to Makkah hotels, fleet carousels) progress from right to left in Arabic viewports.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A base-4/base-8 spacing rhythm governs all vertical cadences. Large sections are isolated by `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to maintain an uncluttered visual field.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, sun-diffused ambient depth rather than harsh synthetic drops. The layering philosophy mimics natural light resting upon warm desert stone.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background surfaces (`#FAF8F5`) carry no elevation and define the structural boundary of the viewport.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Soft ambient lift. Box shadow: `0px 2px 12px -2px rgba(20, 69, 52, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px rgba(25, 40, 35, 0.03)`. Finished with a delicate hairline border: `1px solid #E6DFD3`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** Gentle warm golden-green diffusion. Box shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 69, 52, 0.08), 0px 3px 8px -2px rgba(198, 159, 84, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Trip Trackers):** Grounded institutional presence. Box shadow: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(13, 53, 40, 0.16), 0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(25, 40, 35, 0.06)`.
- **Islamic Filigree Layering:** Selected card headers or elevated feature blocks may incorporate a 3% opacity micro-geometric lattice watermarked into `#FFFFFF` surfaces, grounding the layout in authentic architectural motifs.

## Shapes

The shape system employs tailored, soft-cornered geometry that reflects the curved arches, portals, and domes of Islamic hospitality architecture without becoming excessively playful.

- **Standard Elements (`rounded: 0.5rem`):** Applied to form text inputs, small badges, action chips, and alert strips.
- **Containers & Vehicle Cards (`rounded-lg: 1rem`):** Applied to bus reservation tiles, package summaries, passenger dossiers, and dialog boxes.
- **Hero Framing & Special Feature Panels (`rounded-xl: 1.5rem`):** Applied to VIP service showcases and mobile bottom sheets.
- **Pills (`rounded-full: 9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., "Confirmed", "En Route"), passenger count pills, and main primary floating interaction triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Rawadh Green (`#144534`), label in Warm Cream (`#FAF8F5`), border in 1px solid `#1B5E43`. Hover transitions to `#1B5E43` with a subtle desert gold inset bottom border (`#C69F54`).
- **Secondary Button:** Background in translucent gold-tint cream (`#F5F1E9`), label in `#144534`, border in `1px solid #C69F54`.
- **VIP / Executive Button:** Background in Desert Gold (`#C69F54`), label in `#0D3528` with bold weight, hover state `#D8B26E`.

### Cards & Reservation Modules
- Base background is pure white (`#FFFFFF`) sitting over the cream canvas (`#FAF8F5`). Framed in `1px solid #E6DFD3`.
- Card headers feature an optional 2px top decorative border colored in `#C69F54` for elite transport classes (e.g., VIP GMC Yukon / Mercedes VIP Sprinter fleets).
- Padding strictly adheres to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop, collapsing gracefully to `space-md` (`1rem`) on mobile.

### Chips & Route Badges
- **Trip Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Madinah Route Chip:** Pale gold container (`#F9F5EC`) with `#AA8237` border and `#144534` text.
- **Makkah Route Chip:** Pale forest container (`#EFF5F2`) with `#1B5E43` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background set to `#FFFFFF` with default border in `#E6DFD3`.
- Active focus state: Smooth transition to `1.5px solid #144534` accompanied by a soft ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 159, 84, 0.20)`.
- In RTL layout, prefix icons (such as prayer-clock or luggage markers) anchor cleanly to the inner-right edge with `space-md` inset.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px custom controls. Unchecked border is `#C4B9A7`. Checked state fills with `#144534` housing a clean white or gold glyph checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Pilgrim Manifest Stepper:** Visual pipeline displaying journey nodes (Airport Pickup &rarr; Miqat Stop &rarr; Makkah Hotel &rarr; Haram Shuttle). Active stage glows with Desert Gold (`#C69F54`) halo.
- **Haramain Shuttle Countdown Widget:** High-contrast, prayer-aware card displaying live bus departures relative to Adhan/Iqamah timings, styled with deep green backdrop (`#0D3528`) and warm desert gold figures.
